Topic 1

Families

All families are different.

A family is the people who love and care for you. Families come in lots of shapes:

  • A mum and a dad and children.
  • One parent and children.
  • Two mums or two dads.
  • Step-parents and step-siblings.
  • Grandparents looking after grandchildren.
  • Adopted or fostered children.

None of these is more “right” than another. What makes a family is love, not what it looks like from outside.

Topic 2

Feelings

Naming what you feel.

Feelings are everywhere inside you. Some big ones:

  • Happy — light, smiley, want to laugh.
  • Sad — heavy, want to cry.
  • Angry — hot, fizzing, want to shout.
  • Scared — shivery, want to hide.
  • Excited — jumpy, can’t sit still.
  • Worried — tight in the tummy.

All feelings are OK. The important thing is what you DO with them. If you’re angry, talk to someone instead of hitting. If you’re sad, ask for a cuddle. If you’re worried, tell a grown-up.

Topic 3

Friends

What makes a good friend?

A good friend:

  • Listens to you.
  • Plays fairly.
  • Says sorry when they’re wrong.
  • Shares.
  • Sticks up for you.
  • Lets you have other friends too.

If a friend is unkind a lot of the time, that isn’t really a friend. It’s OK to play with someone else. It’s OK to tell a grown-up.

Topic 4

Being Safe

Your body, your rules.

Your body belongs to you. No one is allowed to touch you in a way that makes you uncomfortable. The NSPCC has a brilliant rule called PANTS:

  • Privates are private. The bits covered by your underwear are yours and no one else’s.
  • Always remember your body belongs to you.
  • No means no.
  • Talk about secrets that upset you.
  • Speak up, someone can help.

If anyone makes you feel unsafe — even someone you know, even an adult — you can ALWAYS tell another trusted grown-up. You won’t be in trouble. You’ll be helped.

Topic 5

Looking After Yourself

Hygiene & healthy habits.

Looking after your body keeps you well:

  • Wash your hands — before food, after the loo, after sneezing.
  • Brush your teeth — morning and bedtime, two minutes.
  • Have a bath or shower — keeps skin clean and you smelling nice.
  • Drink water — your body needs it all day.
  • Sleep — little kids need 10–11 hours a night.
  • Eat a rainbow — lots of different colours of fruit and veg.
  • Move — run, jump, play. Bodies are built to move.
