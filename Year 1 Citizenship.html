Topic 1

Why We Have Rules

Rules keep us safe and fair.

Imagine a game with no rules. Footy where you could just pick up the ball. Snakes & Ladders where you could climb up snakes too. The game would fall apart.

Rules keep things safe and fair. At school, rules help everyone learn. At home, rules help everyone get on. On the road, rules stop crashes.

Some rules are written down (laws). Some are unwritten (we just know them — like saying please and thank you). Both kinds matter.

Topic 2

Our School Community

We all belong here.

Your school is a small community — a group of people who spend time together and look after each other.

It takes lots of people to make school work:

  • Pupils — you!
  • Teachers and teaching assistants.
  • The headteacher — in charge of the whole school.
  • Office staff, dinner ladies, caretakers, cleaners.
  • Crossing patrols, school nurses, governors.

Everyone has a job that helps the school run. Saying thank you to all of them matters.

Topic 3

Being Fair

Treating people equally.

Being fair means giving everyone a chance. If you bring sweets in for your birthday and only share them with two friends, that’s not fair. If you take all the toys out at playtime, that’s not fair either.

Fair doesn’t always mean the SAME. If your friend can’t see well, they might need to sit at the front — even though you’d like to. That’s still fair. Fair means everyone gets what they NEED.

Topic 4

Helping Others

Small things matter.

You don’t have to be a grown-up to help. Even small things make a big difference:

  • Holding a door open.
  • Picking up litter.
  • Sitting with a child who looks lonely.
  • Helping a smaller child tie their shoes.
  • Tidying up without being asked.

Helping doesn’t need a reward. The good feeling is the reward.

Topic 5

We Are All Different

And that’s a good thing.

Look around your classroom. Everyone is different. Different hair, different skin, different families, different homes, different favourite foods, different things they’re good at.

That’s brilliant. If everyone was the same, the world would be very boring. We’re also LOTS the same: we all want to be loved, to feel safe, to play, to be happy.

Notice differences. Be curious. Don’t tease people for being different. That’s a hugely important rule.
