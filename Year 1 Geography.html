Topic 1

Our School & Area

Finding our way around where we live.

Your school is on a street, in a town or village. Near your school there might be shops, a park, a library, a post office.

Your home is also on a street. The streets join together to make a map of your area.

Topic 2

The Four Countries

The UK is four countries in one.

The United Kingdom is made of four countries:

  • England (capital: London)
  • Scotland (capital: Edinburgh)
  • Wales (capital: Cardiff)
  • Northern Ireland (capital: Belfast)

All around the UK is the sea.

Topic 3

Hot and Cold Places

Some places are hot. Some places are freezing.

The Earth has very hot places near the middle, called the tropics. Animals like camels, lions and elephants live there.

The Earth has very cold places at the top and bottom, called the poles. Polar bears live at the North Pole. Penguins live near the South Pole.

The UK is in the middle — not super hot, not super cold.

Topic 4

Weather

What is the sky doing today?

Weather is what it is like outside right now:

  • Sunny ☀
  • Rainy 🌧
  • Cloudy ☁
  • Snowy ❄
  • Windy 🌬
  • Foggy
Topic 5

Things We See in the World

Rivers, mountains, beaches.

Natural things in the world:

  • Mountain — a very tall piece of land, often with snow on top.
  • Beach — sand or stones next to the sea.
  • River — water that flows all the way from the hills to the sea.
  • Forest — a big place with lots of trees.
  • Sea — a huge area of salty water.
