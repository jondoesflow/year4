Topic 1

Mastering Techniques

Putting it all together.

By Year 6 you’ve tried drawing, painting, printing, sculpture, collage, digital and more. This year is about combining skills and developing your own style.

A good Year 6 project might: plan ideas in a sketchbook, make preliminary sketches, decide on materials, make the final piece, and then evaluate — what worked, what didn’t, what you’d do differently.

The process matters as much as the finished piece. Real artists keep notes, try things, fail sometimes, and learn.

Topic 2

Art Movements

Big styles through history.

Art has gone through many movements — styles that big groups of artists shared at one moment in history:

  • Renaissance (1400s-1500s) — realistic, detailed, religious and human themes. Leonardo, Michelangelo.
  • Impressionism (1860s+) — quick, loose brushwork, capturing a moment or a light effect. Monet, Renoir.
  • Cubism (1907+) — breaking up objects into geometric shapes. Picasso, Braque.
  • Surrealism (1920s+) — dreamlike, weird, impossible scenes. Dalí, Magritte.
  • Pop Art (1950s+) — bright, bold, uses images from everyday life. Warhol, Lichtenstein.
  • Abstract Expressionism — paint as pure expression. Jackson Pollock (drip paintings).
Topic 3

Mixed Media

Combining materials.

Mixed media is a piece that uses more than one technique or material. A painting with bits of collage stuck on. A drawing with some watercolour wash. A sculpture with printed textures.

Mixing materials lets you do things neither one could do alone. Ink is brilliant for fine lines but doesn’t cover big areas. Watercolour washes a big background fast. Put them together for the best of both.

Topic 4

Critique

Looking carefully at art.

By Year 6 we don’t just MAKE art — we also talk about it. Looking carefully at a piece of art and discussing it is called a critique.

Questions to ask when you look at any artwork:

  • What do you SEE? Colours, shapes, subject.
  • How does it make you FEEL? Happy, sad, confused, excited?
  • What technique did the artist use? Drawing, painting, collage, sculpture?
  • What do you THINK the artist wanted to say?
  • Do you LIKE it? Why or why not?
Topic 5

Artists

A final trio.

Year 6 artists:

  • Salvador Dalí (1904 – 1989) — Spanish. The king of Surrealism. Melting clocks, strange landscapes. Famous waxed moustache.
  • Jackson Pollock (1912 – 1956) — American. Painted huge canvases on the floor, dripping and flicking paint all over them.
  • Banksy (born roughly 1970s, identity secret) — British street artist. Political and funny stencilled images on walls around the world.
