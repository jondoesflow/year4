Topic 1

Scales & Keys

The building blocks of melody.

A scale is a row of notes played in order. The most famous is the C major scale: C D E F G A B C. Sing “Do Re Mi Fa Sol La Ti Do” and that’s it.

A major scale sounds cheerful. A minor scale sounds sad or mysterious. Most songs you know are in a key — they’re based on a specific scale. “Happy Birthday” is in a major key. “Greensleeves” is in a minor key.

Sharps (#) raise a note by one step. Flats (♭) lower a note by one step.

Topic 2

Improvising

Making music up on the spot.

Improvising is making up music as you go, live. Jazz musicians do it all the time. Rappers freestyling are improvising with words.

Tips for improvising:

  • Stick to a scale so it sounds in key.
  • Start small — just 3 or 4 notes.
  • Listen to the backing. Your notes should fit.
  • Don’t be afraid of silence. Gaps make the phrases that surround them more interesting.
Topic 3

Genres of Music

Different styles around the world.

A genre is a category of music. There are hundreds, but some big ones:

  • Classical — orchestras, no words, from 300+ years ago to today.
  • Jazz — started in African-American communities in early 1900s. Improvisation, swing rhythms.
  • Rock — electric guitars, drums, loud. The Beatles, Queen, Led Zeppelin.
  • Pop — catchy, short, made for the charts. Taylor Swift, Ed Sheeran.
  • Hip-hop — rapping over beats. Started in the Bronx, NYC, in the 1970s.
  • Electronic — computers and synthesisers. House, techno, EDM.
  • Folk — traditional songs passed down through generations.
Topic 4

Composition

Writing complete pieces.

By Year 5 you can write a complete short piece with a clear structure. A common song shape:

  • Verse — tells the story.
  • Chorus — the catchy bit everyone sings along to. Repeats.
  • Bridge — something different to keep things fresh.

Write down your ideas as you go. You don’t have to read music to compose — you can record your voice, draw diagrams, or use software like GarageBand.

Topic 5

Famous Musicians

Three different genres.

Year 5 musicians:

  • Louis Armstrong (1901-1971) — American jazz trumpeter and singer. His gravelly voice and virtuoso trumpet shaped jazz.
  • The Beatles (1960s) — British band. Probably the most famous and influential pop-rock band ever. John, Paul, George, Ringo.
  • Stormzy (born 1993) — British grime/rap artist. Songs about London, faith, politics.
