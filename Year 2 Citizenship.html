Topic 1

Our Local Community

The people around us.

You belong to bigger and bigger groups. Your family is the smallest. Then school. Then your street. Then your town or village. Then your country. Then the whole world.

Your local community includes:

  • Neighbours.
  • Shopkeepers.
  • The doctor and nurses at the GP.
  • The police.
  • Firefighters.
  • Bus and post-van drivers.
  • Park keepers and bin collectors.

All these people make where you live work. We say thank you. We treat them kindly.

Topic 2

People Who Help Us

Emergency services and more.

If something goes really wrong, you can dial 999 in the UK and ask for the right service:

  • Police — if someone’s been hurt or there’s a crime.
  • Ambulance — if someone is very ill or hurt.
  • Fire — if there’s a fire.
  • Coastguard — if someone’s in trouble at sea.

You only call 999 in a real emergency. For things that aren’t emergencies, the number is 111 (NHS) or 101 (police).

Topic 3

Looking After Our World

Small actions, big difference.

The Earth is our shared home. Looking after it is everyone’s job — even small humans!

Things we can do:

  • Put litter in the bin (or take it home if no bin).
  • Recycle paper, plastic and cans.
  • Turn lights off when leaving a room.
  • Don’t leave taps running.
  • Walk or cycle when you can, instead of car.
  • Look after wildlife — don’t pull leaves, don’t scare birds.
Topic 4

Belonging

To groups, places and people.

You belong to lots of things at once. Your family. Your school. Your sports club. Your country.

Belonging feels good — you’re part of something. But sometimes new children feel they don’t belong yet. We can help by:

  • Saying hello and learning their name.
  • Asking them to play.
  • Showing them where things are.
  • Sitting with them at lunch.

Soon, they belong too.

Topic 5

Money

Where it comes from, what it does.

Money is what people use to buy things. In the UK we use pounds (£) and pence (p). There are 100 pence in a pound.

Money usually comes from working. Most adults have a job. They get paid. They use the money for food, the house, clothes, holidays. Some money is also saved for the future.

Things you can’t buy with money: love, friendship, kindness, time. The most important things are free.
