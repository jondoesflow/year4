Topic 1

Parts of the Body

Le corps.

Point to yourself and name the parts:

  • la tête head  les cheveux hair
  • les yeux eyes  le nez nose
  • la bouche mouth  les oreilles ears
  • les bras arms  les jambes legs
  • les mains hands  les pieds feet

To say something hurts: J’ai mal à la tête (I have a headache — literally “I have bad to the head”). Swap the body part in: J’ai mal au ventre (stomach ache), J’ai mal à la jambe (sore leg).

Topic 2

Two Key Verbs

Être & Avoir.

Two French verbs appear EVERYWHERE. Learn them and half of French opens up.

être — to be:

  • je suis I am  tu es you are
  • il est he is  elle est she is
  • nous sommes we are  vous êtes you (plural) are
  • ils/elles sont they are

avoir — to have:

  • j’ai I have  tu as you have
  • il/elle a he/she has
  • nous avons we have  vous avez you have
  • ils/elles ont they have

Remember — for age you use AVOIR: j’ai dix ans (I HAVE ten years).

Topic 3

School & Subjects

L’école.

School subjects in French:

  • les maths maths  le français French
  • l’anglais English  les sciences science
  • l’histoire history  la géographie geography
  • le sport PE  la musique music
  • l’art art  l’informatique computing

To say your opinion: J’adore les maths (I love), J’aime (I like), Je n’aime pas (I don’t like), Je déteste (I hate).

Topic 4

Hobbies

Les passe-temps.

What do you like doing?

  • jouer au foot play football  jouer au tennis play tennis
  • regarder la télé watch TV  écouter de la musique listen to music
  • lire read  dessiner draw
  • nager swim  faire du vélo ride a bike

Combine with j’aime: J’aime jouer au foot = I like to play football.

Topic 5

France & Paris

La France.

France is just across the Channel from the UK. The capital is Paris. Famous sights:

  • La Tour Eiffel — the Eiffel Tower, built 1889, 324m tall.
  • Le Louvre — a huge art museum. Home of the Mona Lisa.
  • Notre-Dame — an 850-year-old cathedral.
  • Les Champs-Élysées — a famous shopping street.

French people eat croissants and baguettes for breakfast. Lunch is often the biggest meal of the day. Cheese is eaten BEFORE pudding, not instead of it. Shops often close between 12 and 2 so everyone can eat lunch properly.
