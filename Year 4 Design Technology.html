Topic 1

Electrical Systems

Adding lights, buzzers, motors.

Year 4 D&T connects to the electricity you’ve learned in Science. You can now include circuits in the things you make. Battery, wires, bulb, switch, buzzer — and suddenly your cardboard house can light up inside.

Simple circuit ideas for projects:

  • A torch with a switch.
  • A quiz board that lights up when you match the right wires.
  • A burglar alarm where opening a door breaks the circuit.
Topic 2

Linkages & Cams

More complex moving parts.

Linkages are bars and pivots connected together to make a complex motion. Lift one end, and the other end moves in a particular way.

A cam is a shaped wheel that makes a follower move up and down as it rotates. Different cam shapes give different movements. A circle-with-a-bump cam makes the follower hop. A heart-shaped cam makes smooth up-down.

You can use cams in a moving toy: turn a handle, and a duck bobs up and down on top.

Topic 3

Textiles

Sewing and fabric work.

Year 4 introduces textiles — working with fabric. Basic skills:

  • Threading a needle — fiddly but essential.
  • Running stitch — the simplest stitch. In-out, in-out.
  • Back stitch — stronger than running stitch.
  • Knotting the thread at the start and end so it doesn’t unravel.

Projects: a simple bag, a cushion cover, a felt puppet, a bookmark.

Topic 4

Cooking & Nutrition

Bigger recipes.

Year 4 cooking gets more ambitious. You’ll use hob and oven with an adult. Skills:

  • Following a recipe step by step.
  • Bridge and claw cutting technique — safe finger positions for cutting vegetables.
  • Measuring accurately with scales, jugs, spoons.
  • Mixing, whisking, kneading.
  • Baking in a hot oven.

Recipes: bread, pizzas, stir-fry, fruit crumbles, biscuits.

Topic 5

Evaluating

Thinking like a designer.

Year 4 evaluation is more detailed. Questions to answer:

  • How does it perform against the design brief (what you set out to make)?
  • Does it meet the needs of its user?
  • What have I learned from making this?
  • What would I do differently next time?
