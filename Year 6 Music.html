Topic 1

Music Theory

Intervals, chords, harmony.

An interval is the distance between two notes. C to E is a third. C to G is a fifth. C to the next C is an octave.

A chord is 3+ notes played together. A major chord is a root note + a major third + a fifth. C major chord = C + E + G.

When multiple notes are played at once, it’s harmony. A melody is one line; harmony is the depth behind it.

Topic 2

Performing

Playing in front of others.

By Year 6 many pupils can perform confidently in front of others. Tips:

  • Practise until it’s effortless, then practise some more.
  • Warm up before performing.
  • Breathe deeply. Nerves are normal.
  • Make eye contact with the audience (or the conductor if in a group).
  • If you make a mistake, carry on. The audience usually doesn’t notice.
Topic 3

Music Technology

Computers change music.

In the last 40 years, computers have transformed music. Professionals now write, record, edit and mix entire tracks on a laptop.

  • GarageBand (Apple) lets you build songs by layering loops and instruments.
  • Logic Pro and Ableton Live are what pros use.
  • MIDI is a way for instruments and computers to talk to each other digitally.
  • Synthesisers make sounds electronically rather than acoustically.
Topic 4

Music Analysis

Thinking hard about what you hear.

When you listen to a piece, Year 6 expects you to ANALYSE it. Questions to ask:

  • Texture: thin (one instrument) or thick (many)?
  • Dynamics: soft, loud, changes during the piece?
  • Tempo: slow, fast, speeds up, slows down?
  • Structure: verse/chorus? Intro/ending?
  • Mood: what does the music make you feel?
  • Instruments: what can you hear?
Topic 5

Famous Musicians

A final lineup.

Year 6 musicians:

  • Aretha Franklin (1942-2018) — American soul singer. The "Queen of Soul". Respect, Natural Woman.
  • Freddie Mercury (1946-1991) — British rock legend, lead singer of Queen. Bohemian Rhapsody, We Will Rock You.
  • Ludovico Einaudi (born 1955) — Italian pianist/composer. Minimalist piano pieces loved by film directors.
