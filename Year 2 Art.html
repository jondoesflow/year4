Topic 1

Pattern

Repeating shapes, lines and colours.

A pattern is something that repeats. Look at the stripes on a bee, the spots on a ladybird, the tiles on a bathroom wall. Patterns are everywhere in nature and in things people have made.

Simple patterns use shapes, lines or colours that repeat:

  • ABAB pattern: red-blue-red-blue-red-blue
  • AABB pattern: square-square-circle-circle…
  • ABC pattern: triangle-square-star-triangle-square-star

Try designing a repeating pattern of your own. Fabric, wallpaper, and wrapping paper all use patterns.

Topic 2

Mixing Colours

From three colours to hundreds.

You already know the three primary colours (red, yellow, blue) and the secondary colours (orange, green, purple). In Year 2 we mix further.

Add a little WHITE and you get a tint (light pink, sky blue, pale green).

Add a little BLACK and you get a shade (dark red, navy blue, forest green).

Mix ALL the primary colours and you usually get brown or grey.

Topic 3

Your Sketchbook

An artist’s best friend.

Every real artist has a sketchbook. It’s a book you scribble in, try ideas in, stick things into. It doesn’t matter if your sketchbook pictures are messy — a sketchbook is for practising.

Good things to draw in a sketchbook:

  • Things you see around you.
  • Your ideas for bigger artworks.
  • Colour experiments.
  • Pictures of leaves, shells, or anything interesting.
Topic 4

Collage

Making pictures from bits of other things.

Collage is a French word for “sticking”. A collage is a picture made by cutting out bits of paper, fabric or photos and sticking them together.

You can collage anything: a sunny beach from torn-up blue and yellow paper, a forest from cut-out leaf shapes, a face from photos in a magazine.

Topic 5

Famous Artists

More people to meet.

Year 2 artists to know:

  • Pablo Picasso (1881 – 1973) — Spanish. Helped invent Cubism, where people and objects are broken up into shapes.
  • Frida Kahlo (1907 – 1954) — Mexican. Known for bright self-portraits.
  • Andy Warhol (1928 – 1987) — American. Pop Art. Famously painted soup cans and repeated portraits in bright colours.
