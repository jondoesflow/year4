Topic 1

Place Value to 1,000

Reading and writing numbers up to one thousand.

In Year 3 we work with numbers up to 1,000. Each number has columns for hundreds, tens and ones.

In 426: the 4 means 4 hundreds (400), the 2 means 2 tens (20), the 6 means 6 ones. 400 + 20 + 6 = 426.

We count in 50s and 100s as well as smaller jumps.

Partitioning

487 = 400 + 80 + 7

629 = 600 + 20 + 9

Topic 2

Adding & Subtracting

Column method with 3-digit numbers.

Line up the columns. Work from the right. Carry when the answer is 10 or more; borrow when you can’t subtract.

Check your work by doing the opposite: if 326 + 145 = 471, then 471 − 145 should equal 326.

Topic 3

3, 4 and 8 Times Tables

Three new tables to learn by heart.

Year 3 adds the 3, 4 and 8 times tables to your 2, 5 and 10 from Year 2.

Tips:

  • The 4 times table is double the 2 times table.
  • The 8 times table is double the 4 times table.
  • 3 x 4 = 4 x 3. Order doesn’t matter.
Topic 4

Fractions

Halves, quarters, thirds and tenths.

A fraction has a top (numerator) and a bottom (denominator). The bottom says how many equal parts the whole is in. The top says how many you have.

Equivalent fractions: 1/2 = 2/4 = 5/10. Same amount, different slices.

Tenths: sharing into 10 equal parts. 3/10 is three tenths.

Topic 5

Measurement & Time

Lengths, weights, clocks and money.

Length: 10 mm = 1 cm, 100 cm = 1 m, 1,000 m = 1 km.

Weight: 1,000 g = 1 kg. Capacity: 1,000 ml = 1 litre.

Time: read clocks to the nearest minute. 12-hour and 24-hour clocks. Work out how long an activity takes.

Money: add and subtract amounts in pounds and pence. 100p = £1.

Topic 6

Shape & Angles

Right angles and the turns that make them.

A right angle is a perfect corner, like the corner of a book. It is a quarter-turn.

Four right angles together = a full turn (360°).

Perimeter is the total distance around the outside of a shape. Add up all the sides.
