Topic 1

Kindness

Small acts, big effects.

Being kind is choosing to help or be nice to someone, even when you don’t have to.

Kindness can be tiny:

  • Smiling at someone.
  • Letting someone go in front of you.
  • Saying “well done”.
  • Sharing your snack.
  • Asking how someone is.

Scientists have proved that being kind to others actually makes YOU happier too. It’s like magic — you give some away and you end up with more.

Topic 2

Bullying

What it is and what to do.

Bullying is when someone is mean to another person on purpose, again and again. It can be:

  • Physical — hitting, pushing, kicking.
  • Verbal — name-calling, mocking, threatening.
  • Social — leaving someone out on purpose, spreading rumours.
  • Online — mean messages, mean videos. Called cyberbullying.

If you’re being bullied, the best thing to do is tell an adult. Not telling makes it worse — the bully thinks they’re winning.

If you SEE bullying, even if it’s not aimed at you, tell someone. That makes you an upstander, not a bystander.

Topic 3

Change

Things move on.

Lots of things change as you grow up:

  • You move to a new class.
  • Friends move away.
  • You get a new sibling.
  • Someone you love gets ill.
  • A pet dies.
  • Parents separate.

Change can feel scary or sad — even when it’s good. Talk about your feelings with someone you trust. The feelings get smaller when you share them.

Even when life feels difficult, lovely things still happen. Both can be true at the same time.

Topic 4

Medicine & Safety

Looking after yourself.

Sometimes when we’re ill, we need medicine. Medicine helps your body get better, but it can also be dangerous if you take it wrong.

Important rules:

  • Only take medicine an adult gives you.
  • Never take a sibling’s or friend’s medicine.
  • Never take more than the dose said on the bottle.
  • Keep medicine out of reach of younger children.

Around the house, watch out for things that look like sweets but aren’t — cleaning bottles, washing pods, batteries. If unsure, ask an adult.

Topic 5

Healthy Eating

The Eatwell Plate.

To stay healthy, your body needs different KINDS of food. The Eatwell Guide shows them in a plate divided up:

  • About a third fruit and vegetables — vitamins and fibre.
  • About a third starchy foods (bread, pasta, rice, potatoes) — energy.
  • Some protein (meat, fish, beans, eggs) — builds your body.
  • Some dairy (milk, cheese, yogurt) — strong bones.
  • A small bit of oil for cooking.
  • Just a little of cakes, biscuits, sweets — they’re fun but not for every day.

Drink water, not fizzy drinks. Five-a-day means five different fruit/veg.
