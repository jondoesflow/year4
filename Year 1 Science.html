Topic 1

Our Five Senses

Our body has five amazing ways to find out about the world.

Your five senses are:

  • Sight — we see with our eyes.
  • Hearing — we hear with our ears.
  • Smell — we smell with our nose.
  • Taste — we taste with our tongue.
  • Touch — we feel with our skin.
Topic 2

Parts of the Body

The main parts of your amazing body.

Main body parts: head, neck, shoulder, arm, elbow, hand, chest, tummy, leg, knee, foot.

Inside your head are your brain, eyes, ears, nose, and mouth.

Topic 3

Animals

Animals come in all shapes and sizes.

We can sort animals into groups:

  • Birds have feathers and wings (robin, pigeon, penguin).
  • Fish live in water and have scales (goldfish, shark).
  • Mammals have fur and feed their babies milk (dog, cow, you!).
  • Reptiles have scaly skin (snake, lizard).
  • Amphibians live on land AND in water (frog, newt).
  • Insects have six legs (ant, ladybird, bee).
Topic 4

Plants

Plants grow all around us.

A plant has these parts: roots, stem, leaves, flower.

Plants need water, light, warmth and air to grow.

We eat parts of plants every day: carrots are roots, lettuce is leaves, broccoli is flowers, and apples are fruit.

Topic 5

The Four Seasons

The year rolls around in four parts.

Spring — flowers open, baby animals are born.

Summer — hot, long days, lots of sunshine.

Autumn — leaves turn orange and fall.

Winter — cold, short days, sometimes snow.
