Topic 1

Islam

Muslims & their beliefs.

Islam is one of the biggest religions in the world. People who follow it are called Muslims. Muslims believe in ONE God, called Allah (which is simply the Arabic word for God).

Muslims go to a mosque to pray. They pray five times every day, facing towards the city of Mecca in Saudi Arabia.

Their holy book is the Qur’an. Muslims try to read a little of it every day.

The most important Muslim festival is Eid al-Fitr — a huge celebration at the end of a month of daytime fasting called Ramadan.

Topic 2

Prayer

Talking to God.

People who are religious usually pray. Prayer is talking to God — sometimes out loud, sometimes silently in your head.

Different religions pray in different ways:

  • Christians might sit or kneel, hands together, eyes closed.
  • Muslims bow and touch their forehead to the ground, on a prayer mat.
  • Jews might stand, rocking gently while reading.
  • Hindus might light a lamp at a home shrine.

People pray to say thanks, to ask for help, or to remember someone they love.

Topic 3

Festivals

Special celebrations.

Festivals are times of year when people celebrate together. Every religion has them.

  • Christmas (Christian, December 25) — the birth of Jesus.
  • Easter (Christian, spring) — when Jesus came back to life.
  • Eid al-Fitr (Muslim) — end of Ramadan fasting.
  • Hanukkah (Jewish, winter) — festival of lights.
  • Diwali (Hindu & Sikh, autumn) — another festival of lights.
  • Chinese New Year — dragons, red envelopes, and fireworks.
Topic 4

Holy Books

Writing that matters.

Each religion has a holy book — writing that its followers consider to be the most important words in the world.

  • Bible — Christians. Two parts: Old Testament (shared with Jews) and New Testament (about Jesus).
  • Torah — Jews. The first five books of the Bible.
  • Qur’an — Muslims. Written in Arabic.
  • Guru Granth Sahib — Sikhs. Treated with huge respect, even given its own bedroom.

People handle their holy book carefully — wash their hands first, keep it on a high shelf, never put other books on top of it.

Topic 5

The Golden Rule

One rule every religion shares.

Just about every religion has a version of the same rule: treat other people the way you’d like to be treated. People call this the Golden Rule.

  • Jesus (Christianity): “Do to others as you would have them do to you.”
  • Muhammad (Islam): “None of you truly believes until he wishes for his brother what he wishes for himself.”
  • Hindu teaching: “Do not do to others what would cause pain if done to you.”
  • Buddha: “Treat not others in ways that you yourself would find hurtful.”

Different religions, same good idea.
