Topic 1

Phonics Sounds

Letters and the sounds they make.

In Year 1 we sound out words. Each letter (or group of letters) makes a sound.

  • sh as in ship
  • ch as in chip
  • th as in thumb
  • ng as in ring

Join sounds to make whole words: c-a-t, d-o-g, s-u-n.

Topic 2

Tricky Words

Some words you just have to remember.

Not every word sounds like it looks. These tricky words need practice:

the, was, his, some, one, are, my, you, said, have

Topic 3

Capital Letters & Full Stops

Every sentence needs a big letter at the start.

Every sentence starts with a CAPITAL letter. Every sentence ends with a full stop.

We also use capitals for:

  • Names: Amir, Rosie.
  • Places: London.
  • Days: Monday.
  • The word I — ALWAYS a capital.
Topic 4

One or Many

Adding -s or -es.

When you have more than one of something, add s to the word:

  • one cat → two cats
  • one book → ten books

Some words need es instead: bus → buses, box → boxes.

Topic 5

Reading Stories

Books are full of adventures.

When you read, try to picture the story in your head.

Good questions to ask about a story:

  • Who is it about?
  • Where does it happen?
  • What happens at the start, middle, and end?
  • How do the people feel?
