Topic 1

Singing Together

Our voices are instruments.

Your voice is the very first instrument everyone has, always with you. When we sing together in class, we’re making music as a group.

Good singing tips:

  • Stand up straight — it helps your breathing.
  • Open your mouth — the sound needs somewhere to come out!
  • Listen to the teacher and sing the right notes.
  • Keep in time with everyone else.
Topic 2

Rhythm

Keeping the beat.

Every song has a beat. It’s like the heartbeat of the music. If you clap along in time, that’s keeping the beat.

Some beats are slow (like a lullaby). Some are fast (like a pop song). Some are in between. We call this tempo — the speed of the music.

Topic 3

Classroom Instruments

Percussion and more.

In Year 1 we explore lots of classroom instruments:

  • Percussion (things you hit or shake): tambourine, triangle, maracas, drums, claves, cowbell.
  • Tuned instruments (different notes): glockenspiel, chime bars, xylophone.

Hold them properly, play them gently, and wait your turn. Music is about listening as much as playing.

Topic 4

Listening to Music

Paying attention to what you hear.

Music isn’t just for making — it’s for listening to. When we listen carefully, we can ask:

  • Is it LOUD or QUIET?
  • Is it FAST or SLOW?
  • Is it HAPPY or SAD?
  • What INSTRUMENTS can you hear?

Try listening with your eyes closed. You hear more.

Topic 5

Nursery Rhymes & Songs

Songs every Year 1 knows.

Year 1 sings lots of nursery rhymes — the traditional songs that get passed from grown-ups to children. They’re often very old.

Classic songs you might know:

  • Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star
  • The Wheels on the Bus
  • Old MacDonald Had a Farm
  • Incy Wincy Spider
  • Row, Row, Row Your Boat
