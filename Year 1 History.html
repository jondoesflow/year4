Topic 1

Long Ago vs Today

Life used to look very different!

When your grandparents were small, there were no smartphones, no tablets, no YouTube, no streaming TV.

Many toys are still the same! Skipping ropes, footballs, teddy bears, dolls, building bricks.

Ask a grown-up what they played with when they were your age.

Topic 2

Transport Through Time

How people got around.

  • Long ago: people walked, or rode horses, or went by boat.
  • About 200 years ago: first trains with steam engines.
  • About 120 years ago: first cars.
  • About 120 years ago: first aeroplanes (the Wright brothers, 1903).
  • Today: cars, trains, planes, electric scooters.
Topic 3

Queen Elizabeth II

A queen for 70 years.

Queen Elizabeth II was the Queen of the United Kingdom for a very long time — 70 years! Longer than any other British king or queen.

She became queen in 1952 when she was 25. She died in 2022.

Now her son, King Charles III, is the king.

Topic 4

Mary Anning

A girl who found dinosaur bones.

Mary Anning lived a long time ago (1799 – 1847) in a seaside town called Lyme Regis.

When she was 12, she found the first complete skeleton of an ichthyosaur — a big sea reptile that lived millions of years ago.

She kept finding amazing fossils all her life. She helped us learn that dinosaurs and giant sea reptiles existed.

Topic 5

Brave Explorers

People who went where no one had gone before.

Neil Armstrong was the first person to walk on the Moon. He landed on the Moon on 20 July 1969.

Christopher Columbus sailed across the Atlantic Ocean from Spain in 1492 and reached land in the Americas.

Amelia Earhart was the first woman to fly a plane alone across the Atlantic Ocean in 1932.
