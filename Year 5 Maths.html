Topic 1

Place Value to 1,000,000

Bigger numbers, negative numbers, Roman numerals.

In Year 5 we work with numbers up to 1,000,000 (one million).

We also start using negative numbers: −5, −10. These are numbers less than zero, like very cold temperatures.

Roman numerals get bigger too: M = 1,000, D = 500. So MMXXV = 2,025.

Topic 2

Multiples, Factors & Primes

How numbers fit together.

Multiples of 3 are numbers IN the 3 times table: 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18...

Factors of a number are whole numbers that divide into it without remainder. Factors of 12: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12.

A prime number has exactly two factors: 1 and itself. The first few are: 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19, 23.

Square numbers: a number times itself. 4 squared (4²) = 4 × 4 = 16. 5² = 25.

Cube numbers: a number times itself three times. 2³ = 2 × 2 × 2 = 8.

Topic 3

Fractions, Decimals & Percentages

Three ways to show the same thing.

A fraction, a decimal, and a percentage can all mean the same amount:

  • 1/2 = 0.5 = 50%
  • 1/4 = 0.25 = 25%
  • 3/4 = 0.75 = 75%
  • 1/10 = 0.1 = 10%
  • 1/5 = 0.2 = 20%

Percent means “out of 100”. 50% means 50 out of every 100.

Topic 4

Volume, Area & Angles

Measuring space in shapes.

Area = space inside a flat shape. For a rectangle: length × width. Measured in cm² or m².

Volume = space inside a 3D shape. For a cuboid: length × width × height. Measured in cm³ or m³.

Angles:

  • Acute: less than 90°
  • Right: exactly 90°
  • Obtuse: between 90° and 180°
  • Straight: exactly 180°
  • Reflex: more than 180°

Angles on a straight line add to 180°. Angles around a point add to 360°.
