Topic 1

Designing

Thinking before doing.

Year 2 designers take it a step further. We think about materials, tools, and who will use it before we even pick up the scissors.

Good designers ask:

  • Who is it for? A baby? A grown-up?
  • What’s it for? Decoration? A useful job?
  • What should it look like?
  • What materials would work best?

Only when all those answers are clear do we start making.

Topic 2

Structures

Making them stand up.

A structure is something you build that holds up. A tower, a bridge, a house.

Problems with structures: they fall over or collapse. To make them stronger:

  • Triangles are the strongest shape. Bridges use them a lot.
  • Wider bases are more stable. A tall thin thing tips over.
  • Thicker materials are stronger (cardboard tube vs thin paper).
  • Joining pieces properly matters — don’t rely on a tiny blob of glue.
Topic 3

Levers & Pulleys

Simple moving parts.

A lever is a bar that can pivot around a point (called a pivot or fulcrum). Levers let you lift heavy things with less effort. See-saws are levers. Spoons are levers. Scissors are two levers joined.

A pulley is a wheel with a rope round it. Pull the rope, and the load goes up. Pulleys are used on flagpoles and cranes.

Topic 4

Food Preparation

Safe kitchen skills.

In Year 2 we do simple food prep:

  • Washing hands before handling food. Every time.
  • Tying back long hair.
  • Cutting soft things (grapes, banana) with a round-ended knife.
  • Mixing ingredients with a spoon or whisk.
  • Spreading butter, jam, hummus.

Recipes to try: fruit salad, sandwiches, simple wraps, pizza toasts.

Topic 5

Evaluating & Improving

Getting better.

After making, you always evaluate. In Year 2 we think about:

  • Did it do the job?
  • How could I make it better?
  • What would I change next time?
  • What do I like best about it?

Try asking a friend too — they might spot things you missed.
